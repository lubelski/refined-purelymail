# Refined Purelymail privacy policy

Draft for review before publishing.

Refined Purelymail is an independent extension, not a Purelymail product.

The extension applies a locally packaged stylesheet to HTTPS pages on
purelymail.com. It does not collect, store, or transmit personal information,
email content, credentials, browsing history, or analytics. It contains no
runtime JavaScript, background service, advertising, or remote resources.

Chrome's site access allows the stylesheet to change the appearance of matching
pages. The extension does not send data to its developer or any third party.
Purelymail's own website and data practices are governed by its policies, not this one.

Before publishing, the maintainer must supply a public support/contact channel
and a policy effective date, and review this statement against the release artifact.
Any future change involving data handling requires a policy and disclosure review.
